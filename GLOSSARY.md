# Electrifyer

A prank toy that turns a phone into a convincing handheld stun gun: hold the trigger and it fires an electric arc with sound and visuals.

## Language

**Stun gun**:
The handheld device the whole screen depicts.
_Avoid_: Taser, electrocuter, zapper

**Trigger**:
The control the user holds down to fire the stun gun.
_Avoid_: Button, fire button

**Firing**:
The state while the trigger is held; it ends the instant the trigger is released.
_Avoid_: Zapping, active, on

**Prongs**:
The two metal contacts at the front of the stun gun, at the top edge of the screen; the arc jumps between them.
_Avoid_: Electrodes, contacts, tips

**Arc**:
The visible electric discharge between the prongs while firing.
_Avoid_: Spark, bolt, lightning

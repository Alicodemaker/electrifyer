# Electrifyer — brief

## What it is

A prank toy PWA that turns a phone into a convincing handheld stun gun. The whole screen is the device. Hold the trigger and an arc crackles between the prongs.

## Who it's for

Pranksters: people who hold their phone up to a friend, press the trigger and wait for the flinch.

## The problem

Today they use physical gag toys (buzzer handshakes, fake shock pens) or ad-filled "taser simulator" apps. Neither feels real. Electrifyer wins on realism and on opening instantly from a link: no install, no ads.

## Version 1 must

- Show one realistic stun gun as if held in your hand: seen in 3D perspective, trigger end close, with real proportions (about 2.4 times as long as it is wide), on pitch black so the screen itself disappears. Matte black speckled rubber with a sheen that follows the phone's tilt, chrome prongs, grip ridges, "Electrifyer" brand mark. Drawn in code, with no photos.
- Fire while the trigger is held and stop the instant it is released. The spacebar also fires on a laptop.
- While firing, show a jagged, flickering blue-white arc between the prongs. The screen flashes faintly and sparingly: on the opening crack and the hardest snaps.
- While firing, play a sound synthesised live in the browser: crackle, hum and random snaps, never the same twice (ADR 0001). It also plays with the iPhone silent switch on, where iOS allows it.
- Vibrate while firing where the phone supports it (Android).
- Work offline and be installable (PWA).

## Version 1 must NOT

Ads, accounts, scores or counters, a battery or charge game, other devices, a settings screen, a power slider, a safety switch, a camera flashlight, or any interface outside the device (the one exception is a small full-screen button that fades away once full screen).

## Look and feel

Realistic and tactile: matte black rubber, chrome, a pitch-black backdrop and an electric blue-white arc. Nothing on screen breaks the illusion.

## Data

None. Nothing is stored, no analytics, no network calls.

## Success

A friend flinches the first time.

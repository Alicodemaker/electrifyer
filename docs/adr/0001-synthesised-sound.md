# Stun gun sound is synthesised live, not recorded

The firing sound is generated in the browser at runtime (layered crackle, mains-style hum and random snaps) instead of playing recorded clips. This keeps the app offline with nothing to download, avoids audible loops and audio licensing, and lets the sound follow the arc's flicker exactly. Recorded CC0 clips were the alternative; revisit only if the synthesised sound fails the "feels real" test on real phones.
